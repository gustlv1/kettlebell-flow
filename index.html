APP GUIDE

Session — build and run a workout

Set your Equipment (1 or 2 kettlebells), Session Mode (AMRAP or Rounds), Movement level, and Categories, then tap Generate Flow. Each exercise can be expanded for details, or looked up in the Library via its "Full Form Guide" link.

Load · Save · Share — in the "Your Session Flow" heading. Load a saved flow back in, save the current one, or share it as text.
Swap — sits next to each exercise. Trades it for a different move in the same category.

AMRAP mode: a countdown timer for your chosen session length, with a beep at the start and end. You can adjust the time mid-session and mute the beeps.

Mute — shown here unmuted. Tap to silence the AMRAP start/end beeps mid-session.

Rounds mode: no clock — pick a target of 3, 4, or 5 rounds using the small circles in the Round Tracker's heading, then tap through at your own pace. The button's label tells you where you are ("Round 1 Finished", "Round 2 Finished"…), and switches to "Finish Session" on your last round. Tap the count itself to undo a mis-tap.

The yellow tag in the top-left corner of either panel is your weight — tap it to type in whatever you're using, in LB or KG. It's the same value in both modes, and whatever it shows when a session finishes gets saved with that session, so History can show your weight alongside everything else over time.

History — your streak and past sessions

Every completed session logs automatically, in either mode — no action needed. Shows your day streak, total sessions, and a list of recent workouts you can tap into, including the weight you used if you set one. The Export Data / Import Data buttons here back up your history and saved workouts to a file — worth doing occasionally on iPhone, where an installed app's data can clear if it sits unopened about a week.

Saved — workouts you want to reuse

Tap the bookmark icon next to "Your Session Flow" on Session to save a flow you like. Saved workouts show up here, where you can Load one back into the generator any time, or Remove it.

Library — how to do every movement

All 71 exercises in the app, searchable and filterable by category (Hinges, Squats, Presses, Rows, Carries, Core). Setup, step-by-step execution, and common mistakes to avoid for anything you're unsure about.

A few things worth knowing

Everything is local. No login, no account — your history, saved workouts, and streak live only on this phone. It works offline once installed, useful with no signal.
